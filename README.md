Kill Boksoon (2023)

About the Movie
Kill Boksoon is a South Korean action thriller movie released in 2023.

Plot Summary
The movie is about Gil Bok-soon, a professional assassin who works for a powerful organization. She is also a single mother who struggles to balance her dangerous job and her relationship with her teenage daughter.

Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it has exciting action scenes, interesting characters, and an emotional story about family. I admire Bok-soon because she is strong, intelligent, and determined.
